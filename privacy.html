Privacy Policy - Btoan AI

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Btoan AI respects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Btoan AI collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when users use the Btoan AI mobile application and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used by the user, Btoan AI may collect or process the following types of information:

  • Account information, such as username, email address, or login information.
  • Chat messages, prompts, questions, and other text submitted by the user.
  • Images, videos, files, or documents uploaded by the user.
  • Camera data when the user grants camera permission and uses camera-related features.
  • Microphone/audio data when the user grants microphone permission and uses audio-related features.
  • Technical information such as device type, browser/webview information, app version, error logs, and usage information for security and debugging.

2. Camera and Microphone Permissions

Btoan AI may request access to the camera to allow users to take photos, upload images, use visual AI features, scan visual content, or interact with camera-based tools inside the application.

Btoan AI may request microphone permission only when audio input or voice-related features are used.

Camera and microphone permissions are used only after the user grants permission through the Android system prompt. Btoan AI does not secretly activate the camera or microphone in the background.

3. How We Use Information

We use user information to:

  • Provide AI chat and assistant features.
  • Process user messages, images, files, and requests.
  • Enable login, account management, and user experience features.
  • Improve app stability, performance, security, and functionality.
  • Detect, prevent, and troubleshoot technical issues or abuse.

4. Data Sharing

Btoan AI does not sell user personal data.

User data may be processed by service providers, hosting providers, storage providers, or AI processing services only when necessary to operate the app, provide AI responses, store user content, maintain security, or deliver requested features.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Data transmitted between the app and our services is protected using secure communication where available.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

User data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or improve app functionality.

Users may request deletion of their account or personal data by contacting us using the email address below.

7. Children

Btoan AI is not intended for children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
